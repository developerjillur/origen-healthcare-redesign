About Nazira

With more than 14 years of clinical experience, Dr. Nazira Usmanova brings a unique and comprehensive approach to patient care. As a Doctor of Nursing Practice, she holds board certifications in psychiatry, pediatrics, and adult-gerontology, allowing her to serve patients of all ages with expertise and compassion.

Fluent in Russian and Farsi, Dr. Usmanova specializes in treating both mental health and neurological conditions, offering culturally sensitive care to diverse communities across Nebraska and Iowa.

Personalized psychiatric care and Spravato® therapy designed to support your mental wellness and long-term recovery.

Clinical focus

  • Psychiatric evaluations for children, adolescents and adults
  • ADHD and autism (ASD) evaluations
  • Tourette’s and tic disorder care
  • Depression, anxiety, bipolar disorder, OCD, trauma and chronic mental illness
  • Neurology care for headaches, migraines and seizures
  • Medication management and brief psychotherapy

“My passion for healthcare began at a young age. I’ve always been drawn to helping people, and I carry that same compassion into every patient interaction today. I believe that true healing comes from listening, understanding, and walking alongside my patients in their journey to wellness.”

Nazira Usmanova, DNP
