Weight Loss & HRT

Balancing your health and feeling your best often requires more than diet and exercise alone. At ORIGEN Healthcare, we provide personalized weight loss programs and hormone replacement therapy (HRT) to help restore balance, improve energy, and support long-term wellness. Serving patients in Omaha, NE, and throughout Nebraska and Iowa, our goal is to create a plan tailored to your body’s unique needs, so you can achieve sustainable results and regain confidence in your health.

Medical weight loss

Losing weight isn’t just about numbers on a scale. It’s about restoring balance, building confidence, and improving overall health. At ORIGEN Healthcare, we provide medical weight loss services in Omaha, NE, serving patients across Nebraska and Iowa. Our personalized programs are designed to fit your lifestyle and support sustainable results.

With medical guidance, safe strategies, and ongoing support, we help you manage weight effectively while lowering health risks and improving energy. Whether your goal is to prevent chronic conditions, feel stronger, or regain confidence, our team is here to guide you every step of the way.

At ORIGEN Healthcare, we focus on weight management solutions that are realistic, sustainable, and tailored to your body’s unique needs, so you can achieve lasting success.

Injectable vitamins

Sometimes your body needs an extra boost that diet and supplements alone can’t provide. Serving patients across Nebraska and Iowa, these treatments are designed to enhance energy, strengthen immunity, improve focus, and support overall wellness.

At ORIGEN Healthcare, we customize vitamin injections to meet your unique needs, whether you’re looking to combat fatigue, recover from stress, or simply feel your best. With safe, evidence-based care, we make it easier for you to restore balance and optimize your health.

Nutritional consultation

Food is one of the most powerful tools for health and healing. Our nutritional consultation services are designed to help you understand how your diet impacts energy, mood, and long-term wellness. Whether you’re looking to manage weight, improve digestion, or support overall health, our team provides expert, personalized guidance.

At ORIGEN Healthcare, we create nutrition plans that fit your lifestyle and goals, not quick fixes. With compassionate support and evidence-based strategies, we empower you to make lasting changes that nourish both body and mind.

Body scan and analysis

Understanding your body is the first step toward improving your health. Our body scan and analysis service provides valuable insight into key health markers such as body composition, muscle mass, hydration levels, and metabolic rate. This data goes beyond the number on a scale, giving a clearer picture of your overall wellness.

At ORIGEN Healthcare, we use this information to create personalized recommendations that support your unique health goals. Whether you’re focusing on weight loss, fitness performance, or preventative care, a body scan helps track progress and guide effective changes for lasting results.

Non-surgical laser (Cavitation/EMS)

Our non-surgical laser cavitation and EMS treatments offer a safe, non-invasive way to contour your body, reduce stubborn fat, and tone muscles, without surgery or downtime. Cavitation uses advanced ultrasound technology to target and break down fat cells, while EMS (electrical muscle stimulation) strengthens and sculpts muscles for a more defined appearance.

This treatment is comfortable, effective, and tailored to your goals, whether you’re looking to slim problem areas, enhance your fitness results, or boost your confidence. With no recovery time required, you can return to your daily activities right after your session.

Provider consultation

Before beginning any treatment, we offer a comprehensive provider consultation to ensure your care is safe, effective, and tailored to your unique needs. During this one-on-one session, your provider will review your health history, discuss your goals, answer questions, and recommend the best treatment options for you, whether that’s weight loss, hormone replacement therapy (HRT), injectables, nutrition, body scans, or non-surgical laser/EMS.

This consultation is designed to give you confidence and clarity about your next steps, so you can move forward knowing your treatment plan is personalized and supported by expert medical guidance.

HRT (oral, injectable, pellets)

Hormone imbalances can lead to symptoms like fatigue, weight gain, mood changes, low energy, or decreased focus. Our Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) is designed to restore balance and help you feel like yourself again.

We offer oral, injectable, and pellet HRT options, allowing your provider to create a plan that best fits your lifestyle and health needs. Each treatment is carefully monitored to ensure safety, effectiveness, and optimal results.

With HRT, many patients experience improved energy, better sleep, enhanced mood, weight management support, and renewed vitality.

Our goal is to help you achieve a healthier balance so you can enjoy life with strength, clarity, and confidence.
