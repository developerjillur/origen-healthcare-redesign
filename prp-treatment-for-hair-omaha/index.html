What is PRP treatment for hair

PRP treatment for hair is an advanced hair restoration treatment that uses platelet-rich plasma to support natural hair growth. The treatment uses your body’s own growth factors to stimulate hair follicles and improve overall hair health.

It is commonly used to treat thinning hair and early stages of hair loss in both men and women. PRP treatment for hair can help improve hair thickness, reduce shedding and support stronger, healthier hair growth.

PRP treatment for hair does not give instant results in one session. Instead, it works gradually by improving hair follicle activity over time. Multiple sessions are usually recommended for better and longer lasting results.

It is a great option for people who want a natural, non-surgical solution to improve hair density and achieve fuller, thicker and healthier looking hair.

How PRP treatment for hair works

PRP treatment for hair works by using your body’s natural healing properties to stimulate hair growth. A small amount of your blood is processed to extract platelet-rich plasma (PRP), which is rich in growth factors. This PRP is then carefully injected into targeted areas of the scalp.

The treatment helps improve blood circulation to hair follicles, strengthens weak roots, and encourages natural hair regrowth. Over time, it can increase hair thickness, reduce hair shedding and improve overall hair density without surgery or harsh chemicals.

The treatment is done in a few simple steps

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Your hair condition, scalp health and goals are carefully evaluated to create a personalized PRP treatment for hair plan.

  2. 2

    Preparation

    A small amount of blood is drawn and processed to extract platelet-rich plasma (PRP). The scalp is cleaned and a numbing solution may be applied to ensure comfort.

  3. 3

    PRP injection session

    The PRP is injected into targeted areas of the scalp to stimulate hair follicles, improve blood circulation, and promote natural hair growth.

  4. 4

    Follow-up sessions

    Multiple sessions may be recommended to achieve optimal hair growth and long-lasting results, depending on your hair condition and goals.

Questions patients ask

Is PRP treatment for hair safe?

Yes, PRP treatment for hair is safe when performed by trained professionals. It uses your own platelet-rich plasma, reducing the risk of allergic reactions. Mild redness or tenderness may occur after the session, but it typically resolves quickly.

Who is not a good candidate for PRP treatment for hair?

PRP treatment for hair may not be suitable for individuals with certain scalp conditions, active infections, blood disorders, or severe hair loss where follicles are no longer active. A consultation will help determine if this treatment is right for you.

Is PRP treatment for hair worth it?

Yes, PRP treatment for hair is a popular and effective option for improving hair thickness and reducing hair loss. Many patients see noticeable improvements in hair density and overall hair health with consistent sessions.

Is PRP treatment for hair permanent?

PRP treatment for hair provides long-lasting results, but it is not permanent. Maintenance sessions are usually recommended to help maintain your results.

How many sessions are needed?

Most patients require a series of 3 to 4 sessions spaced a few weeks apart, followed by maintenance treatments. The exact number depends on your hair condition and goals.

Does PRP treatment for hair hurt?

The procedure is generally well tolerated. A numbing solution may be applied to reduce discomfort, and most patients experience only mild pressure or slight discomfort during the injections.
