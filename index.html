When antidepressants haven’t worked

Spravato® is an FDA-approved nasal spray for adults with treatment-resistant depression. It is given in our clinic, and you rest in a quiet room while our team monitors you for at least two hours.

Adults with major depression who have tried at least two antidepressants without enough relief may qualify. Your psychiatric evaluation decides.

Questions, answered plainly

Don’t see your question? Call us at (531) 571-3018 and we’ll talk it through.

When should I see a psychiatrist?

If you are experiencing ongoing depression, anxiety, mood changes, difficulty focusing, or emotional distress that affects your daily life, it may be helpful to see a psychiatrist. Early evaluation can help identify the cause of symptoms and create an effective treatment plan.

Do you treat both children and adults?

Yes. At ORIGEN Healthcare we provide mental health services for children, adolescents, and adults. Our clinic offers psychiatric evaluations and treatment for a wide range of conditions including ADHD, anxiety, depression, and mood disorders.

What happens during a psychiatric evaluation?

During a psychiatric evaluation, the provider will review your medical history, mental health symptoms, lifestyle, and concerns. This helps determine an accurate diagnosis and allows us to develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your needs.

Do you offer treatment for ADHD and autism?

Yes. ORIGEN Healthcare provides ADHD evaluations and Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD) assessments. Early diagnosis and proper management can help improve focus, learning ability, and daily functioning.

Can mental health conditions be treated without medication?

In some cases, treatment may include therapy, lifestyle adjustments, or other supportive approaches. However, medication management may also be recommended depending on the diagnosis and severity of symptoms.

What is Spravato therapy?

Spravato® (esketamine) is an FDA-approved nasal spray used to treat adults with treatment-resistant depression. It works differently from traditional antidepressants and is administered in a medical clinic under professional supervision.

Who qualifies for Spravato treatment?

Spravato therapy is typically recommended for patients who have tried multiple antidepressant medications without significant improvement. A psychiatric evaluation is required to determine if the treatment is appropriate.

How long does a Spravato treatment session take?

A typical Spravato treatment visit lasts at least two hours. After the medication is administered, patients remain in the clinic for observation to ensure safety and monitor their response to treatment.

Is Spravato treatment safe?

Spravato is FDA-approved and administered under strict medical supervision. Our team carefully monitors patients during each visit to ensure safety and provide the best possible care throughout the treatment process.

How quickly does Spravato start working?

Some patients begin noticing improvement in depressive symptoms within a few weeks of treatment. However, results can vary depending on individual health conditions and treatment response.

What medical spa treatments do you offer?

Our medical spa offers a variety of aesthetic treatments including HydraFacial, PRP microneedling, Morpheus8 skin tightening, laser hair removal, and skin rejuvenation services designed to improve skin health and appearance.

Are medical spa treatments safe?

Yes. All treatments are performed in a professional medical setting using advanced equipment and medically supervised protocols to ensure patient safety and effective results.

How long does a HydraFacial treatment take?

A HydraFacial treatment usually takes between 45 and 60 minutes. It deeply cleanses, exfoliates, and hydrates the skin, leaving it refreshed and glowing.

What is PRP microneedling?

PRP microneedling combines microneedling with platelet-rich plasma from your own blood to stimulate collagen production and improve skin texture, tone, and overall appearance.

How many sessions are needed for laser hair removal?

Most patients require multiple sessions for optimal results because hair grows in cycles. Your provider will recommend a treatment schedule based on your skin type and hair growth pattern.
