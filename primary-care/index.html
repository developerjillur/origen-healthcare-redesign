Primary care in Omaha, NE

Primary Care

At ORIGEN Healthcare, we believe primary care is the foundation of lifelong health. Our team provides compassionate, comprehensive care for patients of all ages across Omaha, NE, and throughout Nebraska and Iowa. Whether you need routine checkups, help managing everyday health concerns, or support in staying active and well, we are here to guide you every step of the way.

Our primary care approach focuses on prevention, early detection, and personalized treatment. By building strong, lasting relationships with our patients, we ensure you receive care that fits your unique needs and helps you feel your best, today and in the future.

Preventive care

Good health starts with prevention. Our preventive care focuses on routine checkups, screenings, and health education to help you stay ahead of potential issues. By catching concerns early and promoting healthy habits, we support your long-term well-being and give you the tools to take charge of your health.

Wellness

Wellness is more than the absence of illness: it’s about living your best life. Our wellness services are designed to help you feel your best, offering guidance on lifestyle, nutrition, and overall balance. We take the time to understand your goals and create a personalized approach to support both body and mind.

Minor acute care

When unexpected health concerns arise, we’re here to help. From common colds and infections to minor injuries, our team provides prompt, effective care so you can recover quickly and return to your normal activities with confidence. You’ll receive attentive support and evidence-based treatment for peace of mind.

Sports physicals

Sports and school physicals ensure your child is healthy, strong, and ready for the activities they enjoy. We provide thorough evaluations to check overall health, identify potential risks, and give families confidence that their child is safe to participate in sports or school programs.
