Compassionate psychiatric care for your child

Noticing changes in your child's mood, behavior, or focus can be worrying, and finding the right care shouldn't add to the stress. At ORIGEN Healthcare, our child psychiatry program is built around a thorough evaluation, an honest conversation with your family about what you're seeing at home and school, and a treatment plan that fits your child's needs.

We combine psychiatric evaluation, medication management when appropriate, and ongoing follow-up care to help children and adolescents build the skills and stability they need to thrive, with your family involved every step of the way.

Why families choose ORIGEN for child psychiatry

Choosing a psychiatric provider for your child is one of the more personal decisions a parent can make. Our approach centers on clinical expertise, patience, and treatment plans that involve your whole family, not just a diagnosis.

  • Comprehensive psychiatric evaluations by a board-certified provider
  • Individualized medication management, adjusted as your child grows
  • Parent and family involvement built into every stage of care
  • Coordination with pediatricians, therapists, and schools when helpful
  • Culturally sensitive care, including services in Russian and Farsi
  • In person in Omaha or by telehealth

What is child and adolescent psychiatry?

Child psychiatry is a branch of medical care focused on diagnosing and treating emotional, behavioral, and developmental conditions in children and adolescents. It includes clinical evaluation, diagnosis, medication management when appropriate, and coordination with therapy, school, and pediatric care.

Unlike general counseling, psychiatric care for children is provided by a licensed medical professional who can prescribe and manage medication as part of a broader, family-involved treatment plan. Many children benefit from combining psychiatric care with therapy for a more complete approach.

Conditions treated in child psychiatry range from common concerns like ADHD and anxiety to mood disorders, depression, Autism Spectrum Disorder, and behavioral or trauma-related challenges.

When should your child see a psychiatric provider?

Your child doesn't need to be in crisis to benefit from an evaluation. Many families reach out when changes start affecting school, home life, or relationships. A consultation with our provider is the best way to determine whether child psychiatric care is right for your family.

  • Trouble focusing, sitting still, or completing schoolwork
  • Persistent worry, sadness, or irritability
  • Sudden changes in behavior, sleep, or appetite
  • Difficulty with social interaction or developmental milestones
  • Big emotional reactions that feel hard to manage at home or school
  • A first-time evaluation or a second opinion on an existing diagnosis

  • Personalized Medication Management: Treatment plans are adjusted over time as your child grows and responds to care.
  • Improved School & Home Function: Consistent care helps stabilize mood, focus, and behavior across daily life.

  • Flexible access: in person in Omaha or by telehealth.

Questions patients ask

At what age can my child start psychiatric care?

We provide psychiatric evaluation and treatment for children and adolescents. Your provider will tailor the approach based on your child's age and developmental stage.

Do I need a referral from my child's pediatrician?

No referral is required. You can schedule a consultation directly, though we're happy to coordinate with your child's pediatrician or school if that's helpful.

Will I be involved in my child's treatment plan?

Yes. Parent and family involvement is a core part of child psychiatric care. You'll be included in evaluations, treatment planning, and ongoing progress conversations.

Does insurance cover child psychiatry visits?

Many psychiatric services for children are covered by insurance, including evaluations and medication management. Our team can help verify your benefits before the first visit.

Is telehealth available for my child's appointments?

Yes, we offer secure telehealth visits in addition to in-person appointments at our Omaha office,.

What happens during my child's first psychiatric appointment?

The first visit includes a comprehensive evaluation covering your child's developmental history, current symptoms, school functioning, and family context, so we can build an individualized treatment plan.

Will my child's school be involved in their care?

With your consent, we can coordinate with teachers or school counselors when it supports your child's treatment, particularly for ADHD or learning-related concerns.
