Why language matters in mental health care

Fluent in Russian and Farsi, our provider understands the importance of culture and communication, ensuring every patient feels heard and supported.

Patients and parents can explain symptoms, worries and history directly to the provider, without asking a friend or relative to translate.

Care available in your language

  • Psychiatric evaluations for children, adolescents and adults
  • ADHD and autism (ASD) evaluations
  • Depression, anxiety, mood and trauma care
  • Medication management
  • Spravato® treatment for treatment-resistant depression

Questions patients ask
