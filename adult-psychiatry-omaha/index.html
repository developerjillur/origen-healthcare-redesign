Personalized psychiatric care for adults

Living with anxiety, depression, or an unmanaged mood disorder can affect every part of daily life: work, relationships, sleep, and overall wellbeing. At ORIGEN Healthcare, our adult psychiatry program is built around a thorough evaluation, an honest conversation about your symptoms and goals, and a treatment plan that actually fits your life.

We combine psychiatric evaluation, medication management, and ongoing follow-up care to help adults 18 and older regain stability and function, without judgment, and without a long wait for an appointment.

Choosing a psychiatric provider is a personal decision. Our approach centers on clinical expertise, cultural sensitivity, and treatment plans built around the whole person, not just a diagnosis code.

  • Brief psychotherapy interventions alongside medical care

  • In person in Omaha or by telehealth

What is adult psychiatry?

Adult psychiatry is a branch of medical care focused on diagnosing and treating mental health conditions in patients 18 and older. It includes clinical evaluation, diagnosis, medication management, and coordination with therapy when appropriate.

Unlike general counseling, psychiatric care is provided by a licensed medical professional who can prescribe and manage medication as part of a broader treatment plan. Many adults benefit from combining psychiatric care with talk therapy for a more complete approach to mental wellness.

Conditions treated in adult psychiatry range from common concerns like anxiety and depression to more complex conditions such as bipolar disorder, PTSD, OCD, and schizoaffective disorder.

When should you see an adult psychiatric provider?

You don't need to be in crisis to seek psychiatric care. Many adults benefit from an evaluation when symptoms start interfering with daily life. A consultation with your provider is the best way to determine whether adult psychiatric care is right for you.

  • Persistent sadness, anxiety, or irritability that won't lift
  • Difficulty sleeping, concentrating, or managing energy levels
  • Mood swings that affect work or relationships
  • Symptoms of trauma, panic, or intrusive thoughts
  • Current medication that no longer feels effective
  • A first-time mental health evaluation or a second opinion

Questions patients ask

Does insurance cover adult psychiatry visits?

Many psychiatric services are covered by insurance, including evaluations and medication management. Coverage depends on your specific plan, so our team can help verify your benefits before your first visit.

What is the difference between a psychiatrist and a therapist?

A psychiatric provider evaluates, diagnoses, and manages mental health conditions, often using medication as part of treatment. A therapist typically focuses on talk therapy. Many patients benefit from both working together.

Do I need a referral to see an adult psychiatric provider?

No referral is required. You can schedule a consultation directly with our team to begin your psychiatric evaluation.

Is telehealth available for psychiatric appointments?

Yes, we offer both in-person appointments at our Omaha office and secure telehealth visits.

What happens during a first psychiatric appointment?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive evaluation of your mental health history, current symptoms, and personal goals, which helps your provider build a treatment plan suited to your needs.

How long does it take for psychiatric medication to work?

Timelines vary by medication and condition. Some symptoms may improve within a few weeks, while others take longer. Your provider will monitor your progress and adjust your plan as needed.

Is my information kept confidential?

Yes. All psychiatric care at ORIGEN Healthcare follows HIPAA privacy standards to protect your personal health information.
