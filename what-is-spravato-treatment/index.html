What is SPRAVATO® Treatment | How It Works & What to Expect

By Nazira Usmanova, DNP, PMHNP-BC, May 13, 2026

Depression can make daily life feel very heavy and difficult. Simple tasks like waking up, going to work, or even talking to others may feel overwhelming. Some people try different antidepressants but still do not feel better. This condition is known as treatment-resistant depression.

SPRAVATO® is a newer treatment option for adults who do not respond well to standard antidepressants. It is a nasal spray medicine that is given in a certified medical clinic under supervision. It is not a first-line treatment but is used when other medicines do not work well enough. SPRAVATO® works in a different way than regular antidepressants and may help some people feel better sooner.

What is Spravato?

SPRAVATO® is a prescription nasal spray used for adults with treatment-resistant depression. The active ingredient is esketamine, which is similar to ketamine but made for safe medical use. It is only given in approved clinics where doctors can watch patients carefully. It can be used on its own or together with an oral antidepressant, as part of a wider treatment plan.

How does Spravato work?

Depression can change how brain cells talk to each other. This can cause low mood, low energy, and less interest in daily life. SPRAVATO® works on a brain chemical called glutamate. Glutamate helps brain cells send signals and stay connected.

By improving brain connections, SPRAVATO® may help improve mood and thinking. It may also help with emotional balance. Some people may feel better within a few days. Others may need more time to notice changes. It depends on how each person responds to the treatment.

What to expect during Spravato treatment

Before starting SPRAVATO®, the doctor checks your medical history and blood pressure to make sure it is safe. During the session, you use the nasal spray at the clinic under medical supervision. After taking it, you stay in the clinic for at least two hours so the staff can monitor you. During this time, you may feel sleepy, relaxed, dizzy, or a little disconnected. These effects are temporary and usually go away in a few hours. You should not drive after treatment and should have someone take you home safely.

When will I see Spravato results?

The results of SPRAVATO® treatment can vary from person to person. Some people may notice changes in mood or energy after a few treatments. Others may need more time to feel better. Possible benefits include better mood, more energy, improved focus, less sadness, and more interest in daily life. Mental health recovery takes time and is different for everyone. Doctors may suggest continuing treatment for several weeks or longer based on progress.

What are the Spravato side effects?

Like all medicines, SPRAVATO® may cause side effects. Most side effects are short and happen soon after treatment. Common side effects are dizziness, sleepiness, nausea, headache, higher blood pressure, and feeling disconnected. These effects usually go away within a few hours. Doctors watch patients closely during treatment to keep them safe and comfortable.

How much does Spravato treatment cost?

The cost of SPRAVATO® treatment can be different for each person. It depends on insurance, how often treatment is needed, and clinic fees. Some insurance plans may help pay for it if you have treatment-resistant depression. Patients should talk to their doctor and insurance company to know the exact cost.

Conclusion

SPRAVATO® (esketamine) is a treatment for adults who do not feel better with regular antidepressants. It works in a different way by affecting brain pathways that control mood and emotions. It is given in a medical clinic under close supervision to keep patients safe.
