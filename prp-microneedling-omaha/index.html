What is PRP microneedling

PRP microneedling is an advanced skin treatment that combines microneedling with platelet-rich plasma to improve skin texture and overall appearance. The treatment uses your body’s natural growth factors to support collagen production and skin renewal.

It is commonly used to treat areas such as the face, neck and body. PRP microneedling can help reduce fine lines, acne scars and uneven skin tone. It is suitable for many skin types when performed by trained professionals.

PRP microneedling does not give instant results in one session. Instead, it works gradually by encouraging collagen production over time. Multiple sessions are usually recommended for better and longer-lasting results.

It is a great option for people who want a natural, non-surgical solution to improve skin quality and achieve smoother, healthier and more radiant-looking skin.

How PRP microneedling works

PRP microneedling works by combining microneedling with platelet rich plasma to target deeper layers of the skin. The treatment creates tiny micro injuries that help stimulate collagen production, while PRP supports natural healing, improving skin texture, reducing scars and enhancing overall skin quality over time.

The treatment is done in a few simple steps

  1. 1

    Consultation

    Your skin condition and goals are evaluated to create a personalized PRP microneedling treatment plan.

  2. 2

    Preparation

    The treatment area is cleaned and a numbing cream may be applied to keep you comfortable during the session.

  3. 3

    PRP microneedling session

    Microneedles create tiny channels in the skin while PRP is applied to support healing, boost collagen and improve skin texture.

  4. 4

    Follow-up sessions

    Multiple sessions may be recommended to achieve better results and support long term skin improvement.

Questions patients ask

Is PRP microneedling safe?

Yes, PRP microneedling is safe when done by trained professionals. It uses your own plasma and controlled microneedling to improve skin with settings designed to protect the surrounding skin. Mild redness may happen, but it usually fades quickly.

Who is not a good candidate for PRP microneedling?

People with active skin infections, severe acne or certain medical conditions may not be good candidates. A consultation helps determine if this treatment is right for you.

Is PRP microneedling worth it?

Yes, it is worth it for many people. It helps improve skin texture, reduce scars, and support natural skin renewal with long-term results.

Is PRP microneedling permanent?

No, results are not permanent, but they can last a long time. Maintenance sessions may be needed to keep your skin looking its best.

How many sessions are needed?

Most people need 3 to 4 sessions for the best results. The exact number depends on your skin condition and goals.

Does PRP microneedling hurt?

The treatment is usually comfortable. A numbing cream is applied before the session, so most people feel only mild discomfort.
